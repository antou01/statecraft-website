About the Institute

The Bangladeshi state doesn’t lack a climate strategy. It has outsourced the job of having one — to NGOs, consultants, and donor programs. We call this borrowed capacity, and it’s the problem this institute exists to name and reverse.

The Future We Imagine

A South Asia where governments hold the institutional capacity to govern climate risk and public technology directly — not on loan from the organizations meant to be temporary.

Vision & Mission

Vision: States that build, not borrow, the capacity to govern their own future.
Mission: Rigorous, field-grounded research that makes the case for institution-building — not another parallel program.
